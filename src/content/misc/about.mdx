## $ whoami_
I’m **Giuseppe Galilei**, an **AI product engineer** at **Impact AI**.

## Why this website
I have always looked for ways to use technology to **automate** and **improve** daily life. It started with small experiments: flashing **custom ROMs** on a Samsung Galaxy ACE, hiding **NFC tags** under my high school desk to trigger "do not disturb" mode and avoid getting caught by the teacher, and eventually fitting the whole house with professional-grade **Ubiquiti network** gear because, well, curiosity won.

Over time, that curiosity shifted toward integrating **artificial intelligence** into useful products: systems that solve real problems, fit into real workflows, and remain understandable to the people using them.

This website is my place to share **projects**, **ideas**, **reflections**, concise **code snippets**, and everyday discoveries.

The goal is to contribute, in my own small way, to the developer **community**. Many of my projects would not have existed without personal blogs, Medium posts, Reddit threads, and other little internet breadcrumbs left by people who figured things out first.

This is **not meant to be formal instruction**. I’m learning, building, and documenting what I discover along the way. **Consider it an invitation to join me on my tech journey**!

## About this website
This is a static website created with **Astro**, versioned and continuously integrated on **GitHub**, hosted and continuously deployed on **Netlify**.
Drafts are written in **Notion**, exported as **Markdown** and refined as **MarkdownX**.
The typeface is **Inter**, styling is done with **Tailwind**, and the color palette is inspired by Dieter Rams-era **Braun** products.
