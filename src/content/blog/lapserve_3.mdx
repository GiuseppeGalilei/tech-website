## How photos will be managed

On the phone, I’ll use an app called **[Photosync](https://www.photosync-app.com/home)** to upload newly captured photos to a **WebDAV server** (Nextcloud) each time I connect to my home network. I could also set it up so uploads happen only at night and while the phone is charging. Nice.

These photos would sit in a folder on the HDD, waiting to be cleaned of **junk shots** and, whenever I feel like it, organized into miscellaneous or experience albums, then moved to my photo archive, which also sits on the HDD.

Then **Photoprism** catches the new photos from the archive and displays them.

**Why Photoprism**: I evaluated several alternatives, including the Nextcloud Memories app. At the moment, Photoprism seems like the most thoughtful platform: it has one task, and it does it wonderfully. This may be due to the freemium nature of the product, but I agree with their philosophy, which they have nicely [justified](https://www.photoprism.app/membership/faq) too. Kudos.
Something I appreciated, for example, is the attention devoted to [RAW photo management](https://docs.photoprism.app/developer-guide/media/raw/) and the detailed explanation of the results they achieved compared to other options.
Little things like that show the quality of thought behind the project, so I chose them.
